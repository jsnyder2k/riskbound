Floating screen-on windowKeep Lit

Pops a tiny video window out over whatever app you are using. While it plays, the phone treats it like a running video and keeps the screen on.

This is the whole window. Pinch it smaller once it is floating.

Using it

  1. Tap Float the window. The clip pops out as a small floating player.
  2. Switch to Outlook, a browser, anything. The window rides on top and the screen stays on.
  3. Drag it to a corner. Pinch it to make it as small as the phone allows.
  4. To stop, drag the window down off the bottom of the screen, or tap it and press its close button.
Two things to expect. Chrome puts play and pause controls on the floating window when you tap it; leave it playing. And if you close Chrome from the recent apps list, the window closes with it.
